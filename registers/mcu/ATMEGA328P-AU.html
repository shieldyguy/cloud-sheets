BitsFieldAccessResetWhat it does
7:0CALR/WxOscillator calibration: trims the calibrated internal RC oscillator; a factory value is loaded at reset and software may overwrite it. CAL7 picks the low (0) or high (1) of two overlapping ranges, so 0x7F runs faster than 0x80; CAL6:0 tune within the range, 0x00 lowest to 0x7F highest. This oscillator times EEPROM and Flash writes, which may fail if it is set above 8.8 MHz while writing them.

BitsFieldAccessResetWhat it does
7SPMIER/W01 with the SREG I-bit set: the SPM ready interrupt is on, and it runs for as long as SPMEN is clear.
6RWWSBR0Set by hardware once self-programming (an erase or write of a page) in the RWW section begins; while set, the RWW section cannot be accessed. Cleared by writing RWWSRE to 1 after the operation finishes, or by starting a page load.
5SIGRDR/W0Written 1 together with SPMEN: an LPM within the next three clock cycles loads a signature row byte into the destination register; an SPM within four cycles does nothing. Reserved for future use; not to be used.
4RWWSRER/W0After programming ends (SPMEN clear), written 1 together with SPMEN: an SPM within the next four clock cycles makes the RWW section readable again. It cannot do so while SPMEN is set. Writing it during a Flash load aborts the load and loses the loaded data.
3BLBSETR/W0Written 1 together with SPMEN: an SPM within the next four clock cycles sets the Boot Lock and Memory Lock bits from R0, ignoring R1 and Z; clears itself when done or if no SPM follows within four cycles. An LPM within three cycles of setting BLBSET and SPMEN loads the Lock bits or the Fuse bits, chosen by Z0, into the destination register.
2PGWRTR/W0Written 1 together with SPMEN: an SPM within the next four clock cycles writes the temporary buffer to the page addressed by the upper part of Z; R1 and R0 are ignored. Clears itself when the write ends or if no SPM follows within four cycles. The CPU halts for the whole write when the NRWW section is addressed.
1PGERSR/W0Written 1 together with SPMEN: an SPM within the next four clock cycles erases the page addressed by the upper part of Z; R1 and R0 are ignored. Clears itself when the erase ends or if no SPM follows within four cycles. The CPU halts for the whole operation when the NRWW section is addressed.
0SPMENR/W0Allows an SPM in the next four clock cycles. Written alone, that SPM stores R1:R0 into the temporary page buffer at the Z address, ignoring Z's LSB; written with RWWSRE, BLBSET, PGWRT or PGERS, the SPM does that bit's operation. Clears itself after the SPM or if none follows within four cycles; stays 1 until a Page Erase or Page Write finishes. Low five bits other than 10001, 01001, 00101, 00011 or 00001 do nothing.
