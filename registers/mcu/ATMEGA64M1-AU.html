BitsFieldAccessResetWhat it does
7:0CALnone per bitR/WxBit n of the trim for the calibrated internal RC oscillator. This oscillator times EEPROM and Flash writes, so trimming it changes their timing; do not go above 8.8MHz when writing EEPROM or Flash, or the write may fail.
