OSCCALOscillator Calibration Registerp. 31

offset 0x51 · 8 bits · reset 0bxxxxxxxx · access R/W

  • Initial value of bits 7:0 printed as Device Specific Calibration Value.
BitsFieldAccessResetWhat it does
7:0CALR/WxTrims the internal RC oscillator frequency. Reset loads the factory calibration value; software may write another. CAL7 picks the low (0) or high (1) frequency range, the ranges overlapping so 0x7F runs faster than 0x80; CAL[6:0] tunes within the range from 0x00 (lowest) to 0x7F (highest). The oscillator times EEPROM and Flash writes: above 8.8 MHz those writes may fail. Change the value in steps of at most 0x20, since a cycle-to-cycle frequency change over 2% can make operation unpredictable, and hold the MCU in reset during such changes. Calibration outside the specified range is not guaranteed.

BitsFieldAccessResetWhat it does
5RSIGR/W0With RSIG and SPMEN set, an LPM within 3 cycles loads the device signature imprint table byte chosen by the Z-pointer into the destination register.
4CTPBR/W0Writing it while the temporary page buffer is being filled clears the buffer, losing its data.
3RFLBR/W0With RFLB and SPMEN set, an LPM within 3 cycles loads the lock bits or the fuse bits, chosen by Z0, into the destination register.
2PGWRTR/W0Written 1 together with SPMEN, makes the next SPM within 4 clock cycles write a page from the temporary buffer, at the page address in the high part of Z; R1 and R0 are ignored. Clears itself when the write completes or if no SPM comes within 4 cycles. The CPU halts for the whole page write.
1PGERSR/W0Written 1 together with SPMEN, makes the next SPM within 4 clock cycles erase a page, at the page address in the high part of Z; R1 and R0 are ignored. Clears itself when the erase completes or if no SPM comes within 4 cycles. The CPU halts during the operation.
0SPMENR/W0Enables SPM for the next 4 clock cycles. Set with RSIG, CTPB, RFLB, PGWRT or PGERS, it gives the following LPM or SPM that special function; set alone, the next SPM stores R1:R0 into the temporary page buffer at the Z address, ignoring the Z LSB. Clears itself when the SPM completes or if none comes within 4 cycles; stays 1 during a page erase or write until it finishes.
