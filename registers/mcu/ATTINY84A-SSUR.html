OSCCALOscillator Calibration Registerp. 31

offset 0x51 · 8 bits · reset 0bxxxxxxxx · access R/W

  • Initial value of bits 7:0 printed as Device Specific Calibration Value.
BitsFieldAccessResetWhat it does
7:0CALR/WxTrims the frequency of the calibrated internal RC oscillator. Reset loads the factory calibration value; software may write another. CAL7 picks the range, 0 low and 1 high; the ranges overlap, so 0x7F runs faster than 0x80. CAL[6:0] tunes within the range, 0x00 lowest to 0x7F highest. Frequencies beyond the specified calibration range are not guaranteed. The oscillator times EEPROM and flash writes: while writing them, do not calibrate above 8.8 MHz or the write may fail. Change it in small steps, at most 0x20 per calibration; a jump above 2% between successive cycles can make the MCU behave unpredictably, and the MCU must be held in reset during such changes.

5RSIGR0An LPM within three cycles after RSIG and SPMEN are set loads the destination register from the device signature imprint table, at the entry the Z-pointer selects.
4CTPBR/W0Writing it while the temporary page buffer is being filled clears the buffer, and its data is lost.
3RFLBR/W0With RFLB and SPMEN set, an LPM in the next three cycles loads the lock bits or the fuse bits, as Z0 of the Z-pointer selects, into the destination register.
2PGWRTR/W0Written to 1 together with SPMEN, makes the next SPM within four clock cycles write the temporary buffer to the flash page addressed by the high part of the Z-pointer; R1 and R0 are ignored. Clears itself when the page write completes, or if no SPM runs within four clock cycles. The CPU halts for the whole page write.
1PGERSR/W0Written to 1 together with SPMEN, makes the next SPM within four clock cycles erase the flash page addressed by the high part of the Z-pointer; R1 and R0 are ignored. Clears itself when the page erase completes, or if no SPM runs within four clock cycles. The CPU halts for the whole operation.
0SPMENR/W0Enables SPM for the next four clock cycles. Written along with one of RSIG, CTPB, RFLB, PGWRT and PGERS, it gives the next LPM or SPM a special meaning; written alone, the next SPM stores R1:R0 in the temporary page buffer at the Z-pointer address, ignoring its LSB. Clears itself when an SPM completes, or if none runs within four clock cycles; during a page erase or write it stays 1 until the operation completes.
